Building With Us

The Crossroads is a playground for the neighborhood. We are not a corporation looking for employees. We are a coalition of people building a shared world. If you want to help, you don’t need an application—you just need to start building.

Ways You Can Help

  • The Story: Add your own scenes, characters, or legends to the story world.
  • The Sounds: Take our sounds and mix them into something new.
  • The Art: Create new drawings, photos, or designs for the grid.
  • The Experiments: Build new ways to play the game or test out your own version of the rules.

Rules of the Road

Because we want this to be a community project, we use Creative Commons licenses.

  • You keep your credit: Your name stays attached to anything you create.
  • Keep it open: If you build on our work, your new work must also stay open for others to use (Attribution-ShareAlike).
  • Share the love: If you modify our work, you must give us credit for the original parts you used.

How to Start

Don’t send a job application—we aren't hiring. If you have an idea or something you’ve built, just share it:

Build it → Post it → Attribute it → Show us.

If you have a cool project, show us what you made on social media or in our community spaces. Let’s build this together.

The Crossroads is a playground for the neighborhood. We are not a corporation looking for employees. We are a coalition of people building a shared world. If you want to help, you don’t need an application—you just need to start building.

Ways You Can Help

  • THE STORY: Add your own scenes, characters, or legends.
  • THE SOUNDS: Take our sounds and mix them into something new.
  • THE ART: Create new drawings, photos, or designs for the grid.
  • THE EXPERIMENTS: Build new ways to play the game.

Rules of the Road

Because we want this to be a community project, we use Creative Commons licenses.

  • CREDIT: Your name stays attached to your creation.
  • OPEN SOURCE: Derivative works must remain open (Attribution-ShareAlike).
  • PROVENANCE: If you modify our work, keep the original credit chain intact.

How to Start

Build it → Post it → Attribute it → Show us.

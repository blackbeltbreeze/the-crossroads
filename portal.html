The Crossroads is an arts and organizing project based in Montgomery, Alabama. Created by Evan Milligan — organizer, named plaintiff in Allen v. Milligan, and artist in residence at the Sanctuary at Jubilee Community Center — together with a growing team of artist-organizers across the Black Belt and beyond.

Here's what's real and available right now:

🎵 Two albums you can listen to today — Caregiver Road Trip and Smoke & Mirrors

🏛 A live performance series at the Sanctuary at Jubilee Community Center in Montgomery

📰 Writing and op-eds about voting rights, the Black Belt, and what comes next

🤝 A team of real artists, organizers, lawyers, and neighbors building something together

What you're about to read below is the playworld — a fictional layer called The Crossroads that ties all of the above together. It includes a musical called Narissa's Home, a radio station called WZRD 77.7 FM, and a cast of characters set across Montgomery in 2026 and across time. The story is real-life arts work; the characters live inside the story.

Think of this whole site as the playbill for a show that hasn't quite started yet, but already has the band warming up.

This is The Crossroads. A spiritual gathering place where all timelines converge. The map shows Alabama's counties with seven allies scattered across the state. At the center— where Interstate 65 and Interstate 85 intersect in Montgomery—glows Africa itself, transformed into a golden portal.

Below the sacred fence, a sword grows upward through the wood. Trees frame the blade. A boombox sits on the grass. Mojo jars anchor the space. And underneath it all, Bertha— the emerald dragon—coils in power.

The Seven Allies

These are the seven main characters who live inside the Crossroads story. They are inspired by real ancestors, neighbors, and creative collaborators — but they are characters in the playworld, not people you can email. The real team is named in the Playbill.

Tyra Washington

EAST | 2026

The visionary. The one who speaks her truth but cannot name what haunts her. She stands at the center, holding the weight of transformation.

Big Kitty

CENTER | All Time

The gentle giant. Wise, patient, holding space for all. Deep as the ocean. Present as breath. The one who knows what must happen.

Peppaman

NORTH | 1996

The caregiver. The spiritual anchor. Warmth and protection embodied. When he moves through a space, healing follows.

Jamal

NORTHWEST | Throughout

The grounded one. Builder. Witness. Keeper of stories. His presence means the truth is being told and documented with love.

Future McFerrin

NORTHEAST | 2026+

Youth. Energy. Music. The voice that broadcasts change. Covered in Peppaman's blood, the face of the uprising. Sound made flesh.

Bunny

SOUTHWEST | All Time

Small but fierce. Tender and strong. The one who carries what others cannot. Hops through dimensions, never giving up.

Bertha (The Dragon)

SOUTHWEST | 1901-Present

The bound one. Emerald power coiled beneath the earth. The energy that fuels systems of control. Now awakening. Now choosing differently.

"THE CROSSROADS PRESENTS"

At the intersection of Interstate 65 and Interstate 85, in the heart of Montgomery, Africa itself appears and glows. A metal fence. A sword growing upward. A tree surrounding it. Mojo jars grounding the space. A boombox broadcasting resistance.

This is where the story happens. This is where choices are made. This is where old worlds end and new ones are born.

The Timelines

⚡ EAST: 2026 | The Uprising Exposed

Year of Rupture

Tyra Washington stands at the crossroads. An army of friends occupies the exits. A dragon stirs beneath the city. The moment of truth arrives.

🌱 NORTH: 1996 | Sanctuary Grows

Year of Foundation

Ms. Ora tends the wounds. Peppaman holds the center. A place begins to bloom. No one yet knows this will be the anchor point for what's coming.

⛓️ WEST: 1901 | Bertha Bound

Year of Binding

The dragon is bound. A constitution is written to exclude. Power is stolen, hidden, and buried in the ground beneath Montgomery.

🛣️ SOUTH: 1952-1969 | Highway Cuts

Year of Displacement

Families scatter. Communities split. Interstates divide neighborhoods. But underground networks remain. Resistance continues.

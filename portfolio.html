HOW THE MAP WORKS

The 9-Point Map System

We don't tell a boring, straight-line story. The Crossroads uses a legendary 9-point map layout (East, North, West, South, Center, Lower, Higher, and a Zig-Zag path). It functions as:

  • Real dance steps — choreography that moves people in real life.
  • Hand signs — actual physical movements used by the actors.
  • Story architecture — how the entire plot connects across 7 different eras.
  • A tool for life — a simple way for anyone to find their direction when lost.

This Map Isn't Rules—It's a Compass

It doesn't claim to have every answer. It's a way to train your brain.
It forces players to pick a direction, know where they stand, and make moves together.
We've tested it on the ground for 6 years. It works every time.

Why This Rules

Normal stories make you sit still and watch. The Crossroads forces you to explore, hunt for clues, and jump in. Audiences don't just sit there—they move through the story like a massive live game. Only the people who follow the map to the very end catch the wildest plot twists (like how Tyra is secretly connected to the giant dragon Bertha in the deep future).

THE MISSION LOG (6-YEAR TIMELINE)

We didn't just cook this up overnight. This project has been battle-tested on the blacktop and in community halls for six years straight.

2018-2019
The spark. Evan starts testing the 9-point map system with real neighborhood groups at the Sanctuary.
2020-2021
First writing workshops. The sci-fi world locks into place. When the pandemic hit, we went digital-first to keep the crew connected.
2022-2023
Microphones hot. Partnered with Western States Center and Culture Lab. Recorded 23 tracks across home setups and pro studios with 100+ artists.
2024 (SPRING)
"Smoke & Mirrors" drops. The world hears the universe for the first time. We start holding block parties and story circles to gather feedback.
2024 (SUMMER)
Live tests. Hit an elementary school assembly with over 200 kids. They learned the hook to "Shake the Field" in 2 minutes and sang it live. The story works for all ages.
2025
Launch phase. Built the interactive map site, dropped character designs, and established Sol R LLC to fund the expansion into 3 more Alabama towns.
